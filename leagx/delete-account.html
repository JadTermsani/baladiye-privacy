Delete Your Account

LEAGX - Sports League Management App

Your Right to Delete: You have the right to delete your LEAGX account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to do so.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the LEAGX app on your device
  2. Go to your Profile by tapping the profile tab in the bottom navigation
  3. Tap Settings (gear icon)
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm deletion in the alert dialog

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion manually, you can email us:

  • Send an email to support@leagx.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include the email address associated with your account
  • We will process your request within 7 business days
Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Profile Information: Name, email, phone number, profile photo
  • Account Credentials: Authentication data
  • League Memberships: Your participation in all leagues and teams
  • Player Statistics: Personal stats and performance data
  • Payment History: Records of league fee transactions
  • Preferences: Notification settings and app preferences
⚠️ Important: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. If you are a league organizer, please transfer ownership of your leagues before deleting your account. Historical match scores and standings may be retained in anonymized form for league integrity.

Data Retention After Deletion

Some data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Match Records: Anonymized scores and standings may be kept for league history
  • Transaction Records: Payment records may be retained as required by financial regulations
  • Legal Requirements: Data required by law may be kept for the legally mandated period
  • Backup Systems: Data in encrypted backups will be permanently deleted within 30 days

Processing Time

  • In-app deletion: Immediate
  • Email request: Up to 7 business days
  • Complete removal from backups: Up to 30 days

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data:
