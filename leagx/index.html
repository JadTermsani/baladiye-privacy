Privacy Policy

LEAGX - Sports League Management App

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Summary: LEAGX helps you create and manage sports leagues, tournaments, and teams. We collect only what's necessary to provide our services, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and phone number
  • Profile Information: Profile photo (optional), player position, and bio
  • League & Team Data: Leagues you create or join, team rosters, match schedules, scores, and statistics
  • Payment Information: Payment details processed securely through Stripe for league fees and subscriptions (we do not store your full card details)
  • Communications: Messages and notifications within leagues and teams

2. Camera & Photo Library

Our app requests camera and photo library access to:

  • Set or update your profile photo
  • Upload league and team logos

Photos are only uploaded when you explicitly choose to. We do not access your camera or photo library in the background.

3. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send notifications for:

  • Match reminders and schedule changes
  • Score updates and game results
  • League announcements from organizers
  • Team invitations and roster updates
  • Payment confirmations and reminders

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the app.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Manage leagues, tournaments, teams, and match schedules
  • Track scores, standings, and player statistics
  • Process payments for league fees and subscriptions
  • Send relevant notifications (with your consent)
  • Facilitate communication between league members
  • Improve our services and app experience

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider with:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row-level security policies
  • Regular security updates

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment provider. We never store your full credit card information on our servers.

6. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • League Organizers: To manage rosters, schedules, and standings within your leagues
  • Team Members: Basic profile info visible to others in your league or team
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Supabase), push notifications (Expo), payment processing (Stripe)

We never sell your personal data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications
  • Request a copy of your data

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: support@leagx.com
