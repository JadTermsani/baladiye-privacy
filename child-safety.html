Child Safety Standards

Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated: January 7, 2025

Our Commitment: Baladiye has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. We are committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Baladiye strictly prohibits:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or endangers children
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Content that sexualizes minors
  • Grooming or solicitation of minors
  • Any interaction that could endanger children's safety

2. Age Requirements

  • Baladiye is intended for users aged 13 and older
  • Users must be of legal age in their jurisdiction to create an account
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13

3. Content Moderation

We actively work to prevent CSAE through:

  • Review of user-generated content and uploads
  • Monitoring of comments and submissions for inappropriate content
  • Immediate removal of any content violating these standards
  • Permanent account termination for violators

4. Reporting Mechanisms

If you encounter any content or behavior that may involve child exploitation:

  • In-App: Use the bug report feature to flag content
  • Email: safety@baladiye.app
  • External: Report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
⚠️ Immediate Action: All reports involving potential child exploitation are treated with the highest priority. We will immediately remove content, terminate accounts, and report to relevant law enforcement authorities as required by law.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

Baladiye cooperates fully with:

  • Law enforcement investigations related to child safety
  • Legal requirements to report CSAM to authorities
  • International organizations working to combat child exploitation

6. Data Retention for Safety

When content is removed for CSAE violations:

  • Evidence may be preserved and reported to law enforcement
  • Account information is retained as required by law
  • Violators are permanently banned from our platform

7. Employee and Partner Standards

  • All team members are trained on child safety policies
  • Service providers must adhere to equivalent child safety standards
  • Regular review and updates of our safety procedures

8. Contact Us

For questions about our child safety standards or to report concerns:

  • Safety Reports: safety@baladiye.app
  • General Inquiries: support@baladiye.app
