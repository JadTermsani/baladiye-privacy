Delete Your Account

Mooi Café - Loyalty & Rewards App

Your Right to Delete: You have the right to delete your Mooi Café account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to do so.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Mooi Café app on your device
  2. Go to Profile by tapping the profile icon in the bottom navigation
  3. Tap "Edit Profile" to access your profile settings
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion manually, you can email us:

  • Send an email to support@mooi.cafe
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include the email address associated with your account
  • We will process your request within 7 business days
Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Profile Information: Name, email, phone number, profile photo
  • Account Credentials: Authentication data
  • Loyalty Data: Points balance, rewards history
  • Preferences: Notification settings, language preferences
  • Reviews: Any reviews or feedback you've submitted
⚠️ Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your accumulated loyalty points and rewards. This action cannot be undone.

Data Retention After Deletion

Some data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Transaction Records: Anonymized records may be kept for business analytics
  • Legal Requirements: Data required by law may be kept for the legally mandated period
  • Backup Systems: Data in encrypted backups will be permanently deleted within 30 days

Processing Time

  • In-app deletion: Immediate
  • Email request: Up to 7 business days
  • Complete removal from backups: Up to 30 days

Questions?

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data:
