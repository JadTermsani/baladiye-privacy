Privacy Policy

Mooi Café - Loyalty & Rewards App

Last updated: January 29, 2026

Summary: Mooi Café app helps you earn and redeem loyalty rewards. We collect only what's necessary to provide our loyalty program, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and phone number
  • Profile Information: Profile photo (optional)
  • Loyalty Data: Points earned, rewards redeemed, visit history
  • Reviews: Feedback and reviews you submit

2. Camera Access

Our app requests camera access to:

  • Scan QR codes at the café to earn loyalty points
  • Update your profile photo (optional)

We only access your camera when you actively use the QR scanner or update your photo. We do not access your camera in the background.

3. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send notifications for:

  • New rewards and special offers
  • Points updates and milestones
  • Important announcements from Mooi Café

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the app.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Manage your loyalty points and rewards
  • Process reward redemptions
  • Send relevant notifications (with your consent)
  • Improve our services and app experience
  • Respond to your feedback and reviews

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider with:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row-level security policies
  • Regular security updates

6. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Mooi Café Staff: To verify and process your rewards
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Supabase), push notifications (Expo)

We never sell your personal data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: support@mooi.cafe
