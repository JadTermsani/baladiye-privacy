Privacy Policy

Baladiye - Lebanese Municipal Services App

Last updated: January 5, 2025

Summary: Baladiye connects Lebanese citizens with their municipalities. We collect only what's necessary to provide municipal services, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, phone number, and municipality affiliation
  • Profile Information: Blood type (optional, for emergency alerts), ID number for official forms
  • Form Submissions: Data you submit through municipal forms (permits, complaints, requests)
  • Photos & Documents: Images you attach to submissions or bug reports

2. Camera & Photo Library

Our app requests camera and photo library access to:

  • Attach photos to municipal form submissions
  • Capture images for bug reports
  • Upload supporting documents

Photos are only uploaded when you explicitly attach them to a submission. We do not access your camera or photos without your action.

3. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send notifications for:

  • Updates on your municipal submissions
  • Important municipal announcements
  • Emergency blood donation alerts (optional)

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or within the app.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your municipal service requests
  • Communicate with you about your submissions
  • Send relevant notifications (with your consent)
  • Improve our services and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider with:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row-level security policies
  • Regular security updates

6. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Your Municipality: To process your requests and submissions
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Supabase), push notifications (Expo)

We never sell your personal data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: support@baladiye.app
